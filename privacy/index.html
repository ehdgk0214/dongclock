개인정보 처리방침

DongClock은 계정 기반 서비스가 아닌 로컬 Windows 앱입니다. 아래 내용은 Basic과 Pro의 현재 production source를 기준으로 합니다.

1. 개발자가 운영하는 계정·측정 서버

DongClock 개발자가 소유·운영하는 사용자 계정 서버 또는 측정 결과 수집 서버는 현재 없습니다.

사용자가 입력한 측정 URL은 측정을 위해 해당 대상 서버 및 그 서버가 지시하는 검증된 리다이렉트 대상으로 전송됩니다. 측정 URL, 표본, 결과 또는 로컬 보고서가 개발자에게 자동 전송되는 경로는 현재 production source에서 확인되지 않았습니다.

2. 분석·광고·오류 보고

Basic 및 Pro production source에는 개발자에게 데이터를 보내는 제품 분석, 광고 추적, 원격 telemetry 또는 자동 crash reporting이 구현되어 있지 않습니다. 앱 내부에서 “진단”이라 부르는 값은 측정 상태를 설명하기 위한 로컬 정보이며 자동 업로드되지 않습니다.

3. 측정 과정에서의 통신

측정을 시작하면 사용자가 지정한 공개 HTTP/HTTPS 서버로 HEAD 요청을 보내며, 필요한 경우 범위가 제한된 GET 요청을 사용합니다. 대상 서버, CDN 또는 프록시는 통상적인 네트워크 요청 정보(예: IP 주소, User-Agent, 요청 시각)를 처리할 수 있으며 각 운영자의 방침이 적용됩니다. DongClock은 로컬·사설·예약 주소를 차단하고 리다이렉트 목적지를 다시 검증하지만, 외부 대상 서버의 개인정보 처리를 통제하지 않습니다.

Gumroad 판매자 화면과 공개 도움말에 따라 DongClock 판매자는 구매·지원·환불 처리를 위해 구매자의 이메일 주소, 구매자가 제공한 경우 이름, 구매한 상품과 구매일 등 주문 기록, 라이선스 관리 정보 및 환불 상태를 확인할 수 있고, 기간별 판매 CSV를 내려받을 수 있습니다. 확인되지 않은 결제수단 상세정보를 DongClock 판매자가 볼 수 있다고 전제하지 않습니다.

이 seller-side 정보는 구매 확인, 라이선스 및 구매 지원, 환불·분쟁 처리, 고객 문의 대응과 적용되는 법적 의무 대응에만 사용하며 제3자 광고나 구매자 프로파일링에 사용하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

DongClock is a local Windows application rather than an account-based service. This policy reflects the current Basic and Pro production source.

1. Developer-operated accounts and servers

DongClock currently has no developer-owned user-account server or measurement-result collection server.

A URL entered by the user is sent to the target being measured and to validated redirect destinations selected by that target. The current production source contains no path that automatically sends measurement URLs, samples, results, or local reports to the developer.

2. Analytics, advertising, and crash reporting

The Basic and Pro production source contains no developer-directed product analytics, advertising tracking, remote telemetry, or automatic crash-reporting integration. Values described in the app as diagnostics are local measurement-state information and are not automatically uploaded.

3. Measurement traffic

When measurement starts, DongClock sends HEAD requests—and, when needed, a range-limited GET request—to the public HTTP/HTTPS server selected by the user. The target server, CDN, or proxy may process ordinary request information such as IP address, User-Agent, and request time under its own policy. DongClock blocks local, private, and reserved destinations and revalidates redirects, but it does not control an external server’s privacy practices.

4. Gumroad purchase information and Pro verification

According to Gumroad’s seller interface and public help materials, the DongClock seller can access a buyer’s email address, name if supplied, order records such as the product and purchase date, license-management information, and refund status for purchase, support, and refund handling, and can export sales for a selected period as CSV. DongClock does not assume seller access to unconfirmed payment-method details.

This seller-side information is used only for purchase verification, license and purchase support, refund/dispute handling, customer support, and compliance with applicable legal obligations. It is not used for third-party advertising or buyer profiling.

For initial activation and periodic revalidation, Pro sends the public Product ID, the user-entered Gumroad License Key, and increment_uses_count=false directly to Gumroad’s fixed license verification API. DongClock reads product identity, original purchase time, and refund/dispute/chargeback status from the response to calculate license state and expiry. The current source has no separate route that sends the key to a developer-owned server.

Gumroad is the purchase and payment platform and a separate handler of personal information. Its processing during checkout and in Gumroad accounts is governed by the Gumroad Privacy Policy. DongClock does not promise that it can delete payment or tax records that Gumroad retains for its own legal obligations.

5. Data stored on the device

  • Settings and recent URLs: language, display/alarm preferences, and recent entered URLs may be stored in the WebView’s local storage.
  • Measurement reports: JSON files are written to a reports folder beside the executable. They may contain target and final URLs, final IP, HTTP status and header-related values, samples, round-trip timing, estimates, and diagnostic state.
  • Pro license cache: license.dat is stored in the app’s Windows local application-data directory. The complete cache—including License Key, Product ID, purchase/expiry/verification timestamps—is protected with Windows DPAPI in current-user scope.

Measurement reports and WebView settings are not covered by the DPAPI protection used for the license cache. Users can manage or delete these local files.

6. Support email information

If you contact dongclock2026@gmail.com, DongClock processes the sender email address, inquiry content, and any screenshots, measurement reports, logs, or diagnostic files you voluntarily attach. A report or screenshot may contain a URL and query entered by the user, a final URL or IP address, or network information.

Before attaching a file, check that it contains no sensitive URL, query, token, password, or other personal information, and remove anything not needed for troubleshooting.

Support information is used to review and answer the inquiry, reproduce and resolve problems, provide purchase or license support, and, where necessary, handle refunds/disputes and legal obligations.

7. Retention

  • General support inquiries — owner policy: retained for up to 12 months after the inquiry is resolved, then deleted.
  • Purchase, refund, or dispute inquiries and working records separately retained by DongClock — owner policy: may be retained for up to three years after the matter is resolved.
  • Legal obligations or ongoing disputes: information may be retained for the longer period required by applicable law, limited to what is necessary.
  • Information retained by Gumroad: as merchant of record and for its own legal obligations, Gumroad may retain certain transaction records for a separate period. This is distinct from DongClock’s email and working-file retention.

The 12-month and three-year periods are DongClock’s current owner policy, not statements of a statutory minimum. Legal review is recommended to establish any exact statutory retention period applicable in a particular jurisdiction.

8. Access, correction, deletion, and privacy requests

Requests regarding access, correction, deletion, or other privacy matters involving purchase or support data may be sent to dongclock2026@gmail.com. DongClock may request the minimum purchase email or order identifier needed to verify and locate the record, but will not request a password, OTP, recovery code, or payment credential.

After identity and the relevant record are verified, information not subject to a legal hold or ongoing dispute will normally be deleted or otherwise addressed within 30 days, and the result will be communicated. If immediate deletion is unavailable, DongClock will explain the reason and the applicable retention scope.

DongClock may be unable to delete information completely from Gumroad’s own systems. The separate Gumroad privacy-request process may apply.

This policy will be updated if the product’s data flows or operating policy changes.
